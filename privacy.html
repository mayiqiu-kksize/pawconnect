Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

PawConnect connects people who want to adopt a pet with animal shelters in Sydney. This page explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who can see it and how long we keep it.

What information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password when you create an account or log in.
  • Adoption applications: your name, email, phone number, suburb and your reasons for adopting a pet.
  • Enquiries: the messages you send to a shelter about a pet.

Why we collect it

  • To let you log in and see your own applications.
  • To send your application or enquiry to the shelter caring for the pet you chose.
  • To let the shelter contact you about your application.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • You can see the information in your own account.
  • Staff of the shelter you apply to or contact can see your application or enquiry. Staff of other shelters cannot.
  • The PawConnect team can access information only to keep the service running.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is active. Applications and enquiries are kept for 12 months after the adoption decision, then deleted.

Your choices

You must give consent before submitting an application. You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by contacting the PawConnect team.

This is a university prototype: information you enter is stored only in your own browser and is not sent to any server.
